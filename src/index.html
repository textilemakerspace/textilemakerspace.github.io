The Textile Makerspace (2018-2026) has closed due to lack of ongoing funding for student staff. We'll have a memorial gathering sometime in the fall, check back for updates.

If you have a memory of the Textile Makerspace that you'd like to share (potentially for use in some kind of memorial tapestry / embroidery / book, etc.), please fill ou this form.

The GSE Makery has expanded its space and staffing for sewing and machine embroidery.

Our un-staffed space for yarn-based crafts (YarnLab) will be open during regular business hours on the 4th floor of Wallenberg, in the red quadrant of CESTA.
